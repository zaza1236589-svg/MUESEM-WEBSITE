GETTING HERE

Transportation Options

Metro

Sadat Station (Lines 1 & 2)

Fares start and most reliable option. Exit at Sadat, the museum is a short walk.

Taxi / Uber

Available throughout Cairo

Uber and Careem are widely available. Request drop-off at the Egyptian Museum, Tahrir Square.

Bus

Multiple routes to Tahrir Square

Various Cairo bus routes pass Tahrir Square. Check Cairo Transportation Authority for current routes.

Walking

From downtown hotels

If staying in central Cairo or Garden City, the museum is within easy walking distance.

GUIDELINES

Visitor Guidelines

To ensure the preservation of these irreplaceable artifacts and a respectful experience for all visitors, please observe the following guidelines during your visit.

Respect all artifacts — touching objects is strictly prohibited

Maintain quiet and decorum in the Royal Mummies Hall

No food or drink inside the exhibition halls

Large bags must be deposited at the cloakroom

Children under 12 must be accompanied by an adult

No flash photography in any hall

Wear comfortable shoes — floors are stone and the museum is large

Appropriate dress is recommended as a cultural consideration
