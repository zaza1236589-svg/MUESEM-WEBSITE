Welcome

A Living Archive

of Human Civilization

Founded in 1902 by the Egyptian government with the guidance of the French Egyptologist Gaston Maspero, the Egyptian Museum in Cairo stands as the oldest and most important museum of ancient Egyptian art in the world.

The neoclassical building on Tahrir Square houses more than 170,000 artifacts — the largest collection of Pharaonic antiquities on Earth. Each object is a window into a civilization that shaped human history.
