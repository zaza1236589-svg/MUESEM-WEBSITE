The Rulers of Egypt

Pharaohs of
the Nile

For over three thousand years, the Pharaohs ruled the Nile Valley as divine kings — intermediaries between gods and humanity. Their reigns shaped the ancient world's greatest civilization, from the pyramid-builders of the Old Kingdom to the last Ptolemaic rulers. The Egyptian Museum preserves artifacts from across this extraordinary continuum of royal power.
